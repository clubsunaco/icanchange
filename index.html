        How this works

        Tropical zodiac and whole sign houses. Planet positions come from Astronomy Engine, a full planetary ephemeris calculated in your browser, accurate to well under an arcminute.

        Everything on this page runs on solar return years: each year starts at the exact moment the Sun returns to its natal degree, and the solar return chart is cast for your birthplace. Profections advance at each solar return. Decennials follow Vettius Valens: 10-year 9-month major periods starting from the sect light, with sub-periods of each planet’s minor years in months, in zodiacal order from the sect light. Bounds are the Egyptian bounds from Ptolemy’s Tetrabiblos. Decans are the Chaldean faces, matched to the Golden Dawn minor arcana, the system T. Susan Chang and Austin Coppock work with. The profected rising degree, the solar return rising degree and Moon, the lord of the year, and each exact transit are read through their bound and decan. The everyday decan lines are written from the Golden Dawn card titles, not quoted from those authors. Profections lead: the profection lords, and any natal or solar return planet in the profected sign, are the year’s focus, and the condition of the lord of the year in the solar return (house, angularity, retrograde, sect) is read first. Uranus, Neptune and Pluto never count as main testimonies: they only appear as small background notes, their map lines are drawn fainter, and the solar return Moon’s next aspect and planetary returns use the seven traditional planets only. Further testimonies come from decennial lords, the ruler of the solar return rising sign, angular solar return planets, solar return planets aspecting natal planets within 3° (conjunction, sextile, square, trine, opposition), planetary returns to the natal sign, the solar return Moon’s next applying aspect, and the solar return Lot of Fortune (day: ASC + Moon − Sun, night: ASC + Sun − Moon).

        Transit lines show where each transiting planet sits on an angle of your natal chart relocated to that spot (cyclocartography). When a transit line and one of your natal lines run near the same spot, the transiting planet aspects that natal planet by angle there: same angle is a conjunction, opposite angle is an opposition, the other pairs are squares. Parans are natal only and not used for transits. The Moon is left out of transit timelines because it passes every place each month.
